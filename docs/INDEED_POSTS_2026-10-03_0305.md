# Indeed posts — independent pros (Detroit metro)

## Before you post: settings for all three

| Indeed field | What to choose |
|---|---|
| Job type | **Contract** (not Full-time or Part-time) |
| Schedule | Leave blank, or "Choose your own hours" |
| Location | Detroit, MI. Set "Remote: No". Add Dearborn, Southfield, Warren and Livonia if Indeed lets you list several cities. |
| Pay | Use the range given under each post, shown "per hour". The post explains it's an estimate and pay is per job. |
| Benefits | Leave unchecked. Contractors don't get employee benefits. |
| Application | "Apply on company website" → the link under each post (it tells you which post each applicant came from). |
| Screening questions | Optional: "Do you have general liability insurance?" and "Do you have reliable transportation?" |

Replace `YOUR-DOMAIN` with your real website before posting.

**Words to avoid, here and when you reply to applicants:** hire, employee, shift, hourly wage, supervisor, training, uniform, full-time, part-time. They make the work sound like a job with us, which hurts the independent-contractor position.

## After posting

- **Where to see applicants:** Hub → Recruiting shows each applicant with the post they came from (`utm_campaign`).
- **Replies:** send interested people the same link, so they're tracked too.
- **Turn on auto-invites** in Hub → Recruiting. Strong applicants then get their setup link automatically, usually within minutes.
- **Keep pay labeled as estimates** in every post and reply: never promise a weekly or hourly amount.
- **If the prices change:** the pay examples are today's suggested prices. If you adjust prices in Hub → Market pricing, update the numbers in the posts.
