# RFP / RFQ archive and resubmission

**Everything is kept.**
- Bids are never deleted.
- Uploading a new version of a file never overwrites the old one.
- Every submission is saved as a permanent, numbered version.

## Find an old RFP or RFQ
Go to **Hub → Bids → Archive**. You can search by title, agency or solicitation number, and filter by type (RFQ, RFP, IFB / ITB, RFI, Sources sought), status and year. Open a bid to see everything about it.

## Files and versions
- In **2. Documents**, use **Upload new version** on any file: the solicitation, an addendum, the price form or our draft response.
- Write what changed, for example "Addendum 2 prices" or "signed Form B".
- The new file becomes current. Earlier versions stay under **Earlier versions** and can still be opened.
- Files that were part of a submission can't be deleted, only replaced with a new version.

## What exactly did we send?
**7. Submissions & versions** lists every time the bid was submitted. Each entry shows the version number, date and time, who submitted it, the total, and why it was sent. **View exactly what was sent** opens the frozen record:
- what changed from the previous version: prices line by line, lines added or removed, and the total
- the full price sheet and pricing assumptions
- the files sent, each openable, plus the confirmation
- the compliance matrix as it was checked, the review sign-off, and the pros' prices behind it

Records can't be edited, even in the database. Use **Print / save as PDF** to keep or share a copy.

## Change and resubmit
1. On a submitted bid, choose **Revise & resubmit**.
2. Pick the reason and say what's changing. Reasons: correction before the deadline, addendum, agency request, best and final offer, or price/scope update.
3. Make the changes. You can upload new versions of files and change prices and the matrix. If the agency gave a new deadline, update it in Details.
4. The review must be **signed off again**.
5. Submit the way the instructions say, then upload the **new confirmation**. The confirmation from the earlier version doesn't count.
6. Press **Mark resubmitted**. It's saved as version 2 (then 3, and so on) with your note and what changed.

## Bid again next cycle
**Start a new bid from this one** creates a new draft that links back to the earlier bid. It copies the details, the compliance matrix (all items unchecked), the price lines, pro costs and benchmarks. Upload the new solicitation and check every item against it.
