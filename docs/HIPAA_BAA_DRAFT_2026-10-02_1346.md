# Business Associate Agreement — DRAFT (for attorney review)

**Between:** [Covered Entity legal name] ("Covered Entity") and [Handled legal entity name]
("Business Associate"), effective [date].

## 1. Purpose
Business Associate provides courier services (pickup and delivery of prescriptions, medical
supplies and equipment, laboratory specimens and records) on behalf of Covered Entity, which may
involve access to Protected Health Information ("PHI") as defined at 45 C.F.R. § 160.103.

## 2. Permitted uses and disclosures
Business Associate may use or disclose PHI only to perform the courier services, as required by
law, or as otherwise permitted by this Agreement, and only the minimum necessary — typically the
recipient's name, delivery address, phone number, and sealed package labels.

## 3. Business Associate's obligations
Business Associate will:
1. Not use or disclose PHI other than as permitted by this Agreement or required by law.
2. Use appropriate administrative, physical and technical safeguards, and comply with the HIPAA
   Security Rule (45 C.F.R. Part 164, Subpart C) for electronic PHI. [Counsel: list safeguards —
   encrypted storage and transport, access limited to the assigned courier and staff, audit logs,
   location data retained ≤ 12 hours, sealed and labeled packages, chain-of-custody log.]
3. Report to Covered Entity any use or disclosure not provided for by this Agreement, including any
   Breach of Unsecured PHI, without unreasonable delay and no later than [__] days after discovery.
4. Ensure any subcontractors (independent couriers) that handle PHI agree in writing to the same
   restrictions and conditions (45 C.F.R. § 164.502(e)(1)(ii)); couriers must hold current HIPAA
   training before any medical delivery.
5. Make PHI available for access and amendment, and provide an accounting of disclosures, as
   required by 45 C.F.R. §§ 164.524, 164.526 and 164.528, to the extent Business Associate holds
   PHI in a designated record set. [Counsel: usually not applicable to couriers — confirm.]
6. Make its internal practices, books and records relating to PHI available to the Secretary of
   HHS for determining compliance.
7. Return or destroy all PHI at termination, if feasible. [Counsel: delivery records retained for
   [__] years for billing; specify protections for retained data.]

## 4. Covered Entity's obligations
Covered Entity will notify Business Associate of any restrictions on use or disclosure of PHI that
affect Business Associate's services, and will not ask Business Associate to use or disclose PHI in
a way not permitted under HIPAA.

## 5. Term and termination
This Agreement runs while services are provided. Either party may terminate if the other
materially breaches it and does not cure within [30] days.
