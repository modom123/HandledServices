# Sales & Marketing Focus: Cleaning in Metro Detroit

**The focus:** cleaning, for homeowners and for businesses, in Detroit and 38 surrounding cities.
Every other service stays bookable; it just isn't what we advertise first.

- **Homeowners:** standard, deep and move-in/move-out cleaning, recurring plans (save up to 20%), carpets, windows.
- **Businesses:** recurring office and common-area cleaning, move-out and turnover deep cleans for property managers, listing and move-out cleans for real estate.

The focus lives in one place in the code, `packages/core/src/focus.ts` (`MARKETING_FOCUS`). Change it there and every channel below follows.

## Cities (39)

**Detroit and inner ring:** Detroit, Hamtramck, Dearborn, Dearborn Heights, Ferndale, Oak Park, Berkley, Royal Oak, Madison Heights, Southfield, Redford, Harper Woods, Grosse Pointe, Eastpointe, Warren.
**Wayne County:** Livonia, Westland, Garden City, Canton, Plymouth, Northville, Taylor, Allen Park, Southgate, Wyandotte.
**Oakland County:** Troy, Birmingham, Bloomfield Hills, West Bloomfield, Farmington Hills, Novi, Rochester Hills, Auburn Hills.
**Macomb County:** Sterling Heights, St. Clair Shores, Roseville, Clinton Township, Shelby Township.
**Washtenaw:** Ann Arbor.

## Check once in the Hub

Saved settings win over the new defaults. If the engines were set up before this change:
1. **Hub → Business leads → settings:** set segments to *Offices, hotels & facilities*, *Property managers* and *Real estate brokerages*, then save.
2. **Hub → Pro leads → settings:** uncheck every trade (to recruit cleaners), or check *House / office cleaning*, *Carpet & upholstery* and *Window cleaning*, then save.

## Each week

1. **Recruit cleaners first.** Marketing can't outrun supply. Aim for enough approved cleaners that every city on the list can be booked within 2–3 days. Watch Hub → Supply gaps.
2. **Google Business Profile:** post weekly (before/after photos, a city name in each post) and ask every cleaning customer for a review. The site already asks after each job.
3. **Google Local Services Ads:** run them for house cleaning across the 39 cities. LSA charges per lead, so cap the weekly budget and track cost per booking.
4. **Email Center:** send "House cleaning in Metro Detroit" to customers once a month, and "Office & turnover cleaning" to business leads not already in a sequence.
5. **Business sales:** call the business leads who opened or replied (Hub → Business leads). Add job postings for cleaners, janitors and porters by hand: the job-posting letter pitches cleaning as a service instead of a hire.
6. **Neighborhood channels:** Nextdoor and local Facebook groups in the 39 cities. Post the recurring-plan offer (same cleaner, save up to 20%).

## What to measure

- Cleaning bookings per week, by city
- Recurring cleaning plans started (the goal: most homeowners on a plan)
- Business accounts opened from the sales engine, and first jobs booked
- Time to assign a cleaner, and cities where nobody is available (recruit there)
- Reviews and rating for cleaning jobs
