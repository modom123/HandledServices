# The Handled Sales Force: Associate AE to VP of Sales

The Partner Program's **15% → 45% of our profit** ladder was always meant to let people grow. This plan keeps that
range and turns each step into a **title with clear targets**, so a rep can see the next promotion in weeks, not at
$1M of lifetime profit. People grow as the company grows: new cities become promotions, the top ranks move to a
salary, and leaders share in the profit they build.

## The promotion chart (everyone starts from zero, everything cumulative)

Commission rises with the money a rep makes Handled, and promotions follow the same number: **the total profit their
clients have made Handled since their first day** (their team's total, for leaders). Profit = what Handled keeps after
paying the pro, on every job their clients book, new and repeat. Total deals and clients won are minimums. Everything
adds up and never resets, so **a rank is never lost**.

A **deal** is a paid sale: a new client, or a new service or site for an existing client.

| Rank | Commission (of profit) | Team share | Total profit made Handled | Team total profit | Total deals | Clients won | Also | Bonus |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Associate AE | **15%** | — | — | — | — | — | — | — |
| Account Executive | **20%** | — | **$5,000** | — | 5 | 3 | — | $250 |
| Senior AE | **25%** | — | **$25,000** | — | 15 | 10 | refunds < 5% | $500 |
| Principal AE | **35%** | — | **$150,000** | — | 40 | 30 | refunds < 5% | $1,500 |
| Team Lead | **30%** | 8% | — | **$150,000** | — | — | 3 reps with a deal; 40 team deals | $1,000 |
| Sales Manager | **35%** | 5% | — | **$750,000** | — | — | 2 Team Leads, 10 reps; 150 team deals | $2,500 |
| Director of Sales | **40%** | 4% | — | **$3,000,000** | — | — | 3 Managers; 500 team deals | $5,000 |
| VP of Sales | **45%** | 3% | — | **$10,000,000** | — | — | 2 Directors; 1,500 team deals; owner approval | $10,000 |

From zero, with $1,500-a-month commercial accounts (3% of clients leaving a month):

| Pace | Account Executive | Senior AE | Principal AE |
|---|---|---|---|
| 4 deals a month (about one a week) | month 2 | month 4 | month 10 |
| 3 deals a month | month 2 | month 5 | month 14 |
| 2 deals a month | month 3 | month 8 | month 20 |

Leaders still have to keep selling to earn on their team ($2,000 of personal profit a quarter); that's a pay rule, not a
promotion rule.

## The leaderboard (last 90 days of revenue)

Rank and pay rates are cumulative and never drop. The **leaderboard** shows who is selling the most right now: revenue
(what their clients paid, new and repeat) in the last 90 days.

- **#1 of the quarter:** President's Club trip and first pick of warm leads next quarter
- **Top 3:** first pick of warm leads from the AI outreach engine next quarter
- **Top 10:** named on the leaderboard and in the monthly sales email

## Three ways to $100K a year (commercial business services)

Business pricing lets Handled keep up to 50% on business work (pros are paid the same dollars as at home prices), so a
$1,500-a-month office account earns about $749 of profit a month.

| Path | Who | What they do | Year 1 | Year 2 on |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Top seller | Senior AE | 3 walkthroughs a week, ~1 new account a week | $56,053 | **$106,971** |
| Big accounts | Principal AE | 3 larger accounts a month | $57,775 | **$109,929** |
| Team builder | Team Lead | 6 reps closing 2 accounts a month each, plus 1 own | $66,990 | **$119,385** |

Assumptions: $1,500-a-month accounts, 1 in 3 walkthroughs becomes an account, 3% of accounts leave each month.

After Senior AE, people choose: keep selling (Principal AE) or lead (Team Lead → VP). Both pay well, and people can switch.

## Independent contractors (1099)

Everyone in the Sales Force is a 1099 independent contractor (owner decision, 2026-11-18). To keep that true in
practice: reps choose their hours, where they sell and how they sell; there are no quotas, schedules or required
meetings; training and mentoring are free and optional, never a promotion requirement; leaders mentor and support
rather than direct; ranks raise commission and nobody loses work for missing a target; Directors and the VP earn a
1099 leadership retainer, not a salary. A 1099-NEC goes to anyone paid $600+ in a year. **Have an employment attorney
confirm this in each state** (MI, WA, NY, NJ, NV): New Jersey's test is strict, and Washington and New York review
contractor sales roles closely.

## Growing with the company

- **Retainer at the top:** Directors $6,250 a month, the VP $10,000 a month (1099 leadership retainer), on top of
  commission, once their team's profit supports it ($250,000 / $1,000,000 a quarter).
- **Profit share:** Directors 2% of their region's net profit; the VP 1% of company net profit; paid quarterly.
- **New markets:** each new city opens a Director and a Manager seat; current leaders get first claim.
- **Ranks never go down:** everything is cumulative from the first day.

## Pay rules

- Paid on **our profit** (price − pro payout − refunds), shown to the rep **in dollars** before they sell.
- The whole sales force together gets at most **60% of our profit** in a new customer's first 12 months. A full
  chain (Principal 35% + Team Lead 8% + Manager 5% + Director 4% + VP 3%) is 55%. Handled keeps every unfilled share.
- **Book share** after year one, while the rep is active: they keep earning on accounts they keep happy.
- **Fast money:** $25 per walkthrough held (up to 20 a week), $100 for the first sale.
- **Paid every Monday** once the job is done and paid; taken back if refunded within 30 days.
- **Not a pyramid:** no fees or purchases; leaders earn only on real paid jobs, never on sign-ups, and must keep
  selling ($2,000 personal profit a quarter) to earn on their team.
