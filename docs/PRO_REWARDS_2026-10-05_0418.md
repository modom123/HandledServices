# Handled Pro Rewards

A loyalty program for independent pros. Points are earned on the revenue they bring in, more for great work and more
the longer they stay. Points are redeemed for gear, gift cards, tools, electronics and trips. They are **points, not
shares or pay**: no securities filings, no K-1s, and nothing that looks like an employee benefit.

## Formula
**points per job = Handled's take ($) × 10 × quality × tenure**
- **Take:** what the customer paid minus the pro's payout. Tips, reimbursements and show-up pay don't count.
- **Quality:** ×1.25 when the job passes review the first time, with no redo or refund and a rating of 4.8★ or higher (or no rating). A refunded job earns 0.
- **Tenure:** ×1.0 / ×1.1 / ×1.25 / ×1.5 at 0–6 / 6–12 / 12–24 / 24+ months, counted from the pro's first completed job.
- **Milestones:** 10 / 50 / 100 / 250 / 500 jobs, 1 / 2 / 3 years, and 25 / 100 five-star reviews (1,000–50,000 points each).
- **Pending:** new points stay pending for 90 days. The final multipliers are applied when they release: refund → void, redo → no quality bonus, plus the customer's rating.
- **Expiry and forfeiture:** points expire after 12 months with no completed job. They are forfeited only when a pro is deactivated for cause (Hub → Rewards → Forfeit).
- **Nothing for accepting offers:** points never depend on accepting or declining offers, or on schedules or hours.

## What it costs
- **Point value:** 100 points ≈ $1 of reward cost (point value $0.01).
- **Base cost:** 10 points × $0.01 = **10% of our take**, about 2% of the job price.
- **Most it can cost:** 18.75% of our take, for a 2-year+ pro who always gets the quality bonus.
- **Watch it:** Hub → Rewards shows the year-to-date cost as a % of take, points owed as a dollar liability, and open orders.
- **Change it:** the earn rate and point value live in Hub → Rewards → Settings. Changes affect future points only.

**Example:** a steady pro doing $2,000 of jobs a week means about $400 a week of take. That's about **208,000 points a year** before multipliers and milestones, or about $2,080 of rewards.

## Catalog (starter, editable in the Hub)
Hat or tee 2,500 · hoodie 6,000 · $50 gas card 5,000 · $100 tool store card 10,000 · work jacket 12,000 · earbuds 15,000 ·
drill kit 20,000 · game-day tickets 30,000 · tablet 35,000 · 55" TV 45,000 · weekend getaway 90,000 · 4-day trip for two 200,000.
Each item's cost (fair market value) is no more than its point value.

## Where it lives
- **Pros, web:** Pro portal → 🎁 Rewards. It shows balance, pending points, tier, rules, milestones, the catalog with redeem (shipping address), orders and history. The pro home page has a Rewards card.
- **Pros, app:** Pro mode → 🎁 Rewards (same screens).
- **Website:** /pros has a Rewards section and a new promise, "Rewards that grow the longer you stay".
- **Hub → 🎁 Pro Rewards:**
  - Liability and cost, and the order queue (approve → order → ship with tracking → deliver, or cancel and return the points).
  - Top pros, catalog editing, point adjustments and forfeits.
  - Settings (admin), and a "Run release now" button.
- **Automatic steps:**
  - Completing a job credits pending points.
  - The daily sweep releases points, awards milestones and expires inactive balances.
  - Pros get push notifications for milestones and shipping.
- **Taxes:** the 1099 worksheet (Network → 1099) adds the fair market value of rewards delivered that year to each pro's total.
- **Contract:** the **Rewards Terms** (pro-rewards-terms), in English and Spanish, are part of the pro contract library.
- **Flyer:** docs/PRO_REWARDS_FLYER_*.pdf (English front, Spanish back). Rebuild it with scripts/build-rewards-flyer-pdf.ts.
