**Account Executive — Commercial Services**
Handled · Detroit, MI · Independent contractor (1099) · Commission + walkthrough pay + bonuses

**About Handled**
Handled is a commercial and home services company: office and facility cleaning, unit turnovers for property managers,
grounds, repairs, hauling and more, done by background-checked, vetted pros with photo proof on every visit.

**What the work is**
- Find offices, property managers, clinics and facilities that need recurring cleaning and upkeep
- Book free walkthroughs (businesses pick a time online from your link) and send an exact price the same day
- Close accounts on the Handled app and keep your clients happy so they keep booking
- Optional: build a team of your own reps and earn on their sales

**How you're paid** (every Monday, by direct deposit)
- **Commission:** 20% of Handled's profit on what you sell to start, rising as you grow to 50%
- **Your book:** keep earning on your clients after their first year
- **$25 for every walkthrough held** from your link, **$100 for your first sale**, and a cash bonus with every promotion
- **Three paths to $100K a year** from commercial accounts (examples on the careers page; earnings depend on what you sell)

**How you move up**
Associate AE → Account Executive → Senior AE → Principal AE, or lead a team: Team Lead → Sales Manager → Director →
VP of Sales. Promotions come from the total profit your clients have made Handled since your first day, plus deals
and clients won. Totals never reset, so you never lose a rank.

**How you work**
You're an independent contractor: you choose your hours, where you sell and how you sell. No quotas, no required
schedule. Free, optional training and a mentor if you want them. We provide the pricing app, landing pages, walkthrough
booking and warm leads from our AI outreach.

**Requirements**
18+, a smartphone, reliable transportation, a bank account for direct deposit, and a basic identity check.

*Independent contractor (1099). We send a 1099-NEC if you earn $600+ in a year.*
