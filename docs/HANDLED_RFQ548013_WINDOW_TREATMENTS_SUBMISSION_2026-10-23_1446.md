Handled Services LLC submits this quote in response to RFQ No. 548013 to supply and install tan/almond, UV-protected vinyl vertical blinds and replacement slats for the Detroit Police Department. This quote remains firm for 120 days from the quote due date.

Handled Services LLC is a Detroit-based services company providing installation, handyman, cleaning and property services through background-checked, insured crews. We will supply all materials, deliver, install, remove and haul away the existing blinds, and leave each room clean.

## 1. What we will supply

| Item | Specification |
|---|---|
| Blinds | Commercial-grade vertical blinds with 3.5" PVC (vinyl) vanes, UV-protected, tan/almond color. A color sample will be submitted for DPD approval before ordering. |
| Headrail | Aluminum headrail with wand control (cordless; no loop cords) |
| Vanes | Bottom weights with connecting chain; vanes cut to each measured length |
| Wide openings | Openings over 144" wide receive two blind units on aligned headrails, so vanes stack cleanly at each mullion. 28 blind units in total for the 20 windows. |
| Mount | Inside mount to the window frame, unless DPD prefers outside mount |
| Replacement slats | 20 each in 75", 48" and 46" lengths, in the same vane, color and finish as installed, labeled by length |

## 2. How we will do the work

1. **Confirm measurements** on site with DPD's permission, and submit a color sample, within 3 business days of the purchase order.
2. **Order** within 2 business days of DPD's color approval. Typical fabrication and shipping time is 1–2 weeks.
3. **Install** within 5 business days of delivery, on dates and times DPD sets: about 1–2 days with a 2-person crew, one room at a time to keep disruption low.
4. **Remove and haul away** the existing blinds, and clean up each room.
5. **Walk through** with DPD's contact to confirm every blind operates correctly, and hand over the labeled replacement slats.

Installers are Handled employees, background-checked, in company-identified clothing, and will follow all DPD building security and sign-in procedures.

## 3. Warranty

Manufacturer's warranty on blinds and headrails, plus Handled's **1-year workmanship warranty**: if anything we installed doesn't operate correctly, we return and fix it at no charge.
