# The Handled Pro Program

Pros are the asset. Customers buy from us because the work is good, and the work is only good if the best local pros choose us over everything else they could do with their week. This document sets out the offer, the vetting, the insurance rules and the recruiting plan.

---

## 1. What we offered before, and why it wasn't enough

The old `/pros` page had four bullets: no lead fees, pre-priced work, prepaid jobs and "we run the office". All true, but:

- **No numbers.** Pros decide on money. The page never said what a job pays.
- **No path up.** Every pro got the same deal forever. There was no reason to be great rather than adequate.
- **Thin vetting.** We checked general liability, a background check and a license for three trades. A tree crew and a dog walker had the same requirements. There were no commercial auto checks for haulers, no bonds for in-home cleaners, no builder's license for remodelers and no specialties.
- **No case against the alternatives.** It didn't explain why a pro should pick us over Angi, Thumbtack or running their own shop on ServiceTitan.

## 2. The offer (live now)

### Seven promises (shown on `/pros`, defined in `packages/core/src/pro-program.ts`)
1. **You see the pay before you say yes.** The exact payout is on every offer. Not enough? Counter with the pay you want — the customer decides. No lead fees, no bidding wars.
2. **The customer has already paid.** Upfront payment means the pro never invoices or chases money.
3. **No lead fees, no subscription, no software bill.** We only earn when they earn.
4. **Paid every week.** Payouts are approved when photo QA passes and are sent automatically every Monday through Stripe Connect, free.
5. **We run the office.** Marketing, quoting, scheduling, reminders, payments, reviews and support.
6. **Recurring customers stay with you.** Every visit on a recurring plan is offered to the same pro first, for 24 hours, then to others if they pass.
7. **You stay independent.** Pros pick their jobs, area and daily capacity, and keep other clients.

### Tiers: a reason to be great
| Tier | Earned at | Perks |
|---|---|---|
| ✓ **Pro** | Activation | Prepaid jobs, weekly pay, no fees |
| ★ **Pro+** | 25 jobs · 4.7★ · 90% on time | **+3% of the job price** on every payout; ranked above Pro tier for offers |
| ◆ **Elite** | 100 jobs · 4.85★ · 95% on time | **+5% of the job price** on every payout; top of the list for every offer |

- Tiers are recomputed from live stats on every dispatch, so they never go stale. A pro who slips drops a tier automatically.
- **The boost can never make a job lose money.** It is clamped so our take never falls below 15%; services already paying 85% get no boost. A unit test checks this for every service at four price points. The boosted payout is written onto that pro's offer, so the pro sees it before accepting.
- **Referral bonus:** $150 when a referred pro finishes their 10th job. Paid automatically by the daily sweep, once.
- **Fast track to Pro+:** a pro with 5+ years in the trade sends 3–10 photos of past work; we review, their next finished job is a paid trial checked by hand (4.8★+), and on approval they start at Pro+ and skip the probation job-size limit. Pro+ is held for their first 10 jobs, then only while rating and on-time stay at Pro+ level.

### Crews
A pro company can list its crew (crew leads, licensed techs, apprentices, helpers) in the portal or app and pick who goes to each job; the customer sees the first name. The owner signs the **Crew Addendum** first: the crew works for the company, which handles their right to work (I-9), pay and workers' comp. Every crew member passes the same background check (Checkr). Licensed work goes only to a crew member with their own license; helpers never go alone; crew can only be sent once a real workers' comp policy is on file.

### How jobs reach pros (live, 2026-10-04_2206 UTC)
1. **First looks** — one pro (or a business's team) sees the job alone for a short window, and can pass at no cost:
   recurring visit (24h, same pro), redo (12h, original pro), a business account's own pros (2h), a customer's
   favorite or the pro they asked for with "Book again" (4h, or 1h when the job is within a day).
2. **Targeted offers** — AI-ranked, 1–2 best-fit pros (2h, or 1h for priority jobs). First to accept gets it.
3. **Jobs near you (open job board)** — anything still untaken 30 minutes after the targeted round shows in the app
   and portal for every pro who qualifies (trade, license, insurance, distance, days, daily limit). City and ZIP
   only until they accept; "Take it" holds the job 15 minutes while they read the work order. First to accept wins.
4. Untaken offers re-dispatch every 10 minutes to the next pros; staff get a critical alert if nobody qualifies.

Customers favorite a pro (or the crew member who came) on their job page and can "Book again with [name]". A
crew member request goes to the company owner, who decides who goes. Never forced; nothing counts against a pro
who passes. Rules: `packages/core/src/board.ts`; dispatch: `apps/web/lib/jobs.ts`; board: `apps/web/lib/board.ts`.

### How we compare
| | **Handled** | Lead sites (Angi, Thumbtack) | Field-service software (ServiceTitan) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cost to the pro | Nothing; we keep a share of finished jobs | Pay for leads, win or lose | Monthly subscription |
| Who finds the customer | We do; the job is already sold | Pro competes for each lead | Pro does their own marketing |
| Price | Customer names it around a market-learned suggestion; pro accepts, passes or counters; payout shown before accept | Pro quotes and negotiates | Pro quotes |
| Getting paid | Customer prepaid; weekly payout | Pro invoices and collects | Pro invoices and collects |
| Office work | Done for them | Theirs | Theirs, with better tools |

**In one line:** Angi sells pros a *chance* at a job. ServiceTitan sells them *software* to run a business they still have to fill. We hand them **a paid job**.

## 3. What each pro does and specializes in (live)

`packages/core/src/vetting.ts` has a profile for each of the 23 trades. Each profile covers:
- what the trade does
- its specialties
- the license required
- any preferred certifications
- the minimum general-liability limit
- extra coverage that is always required, and coverage required only in some situations
- the skills check

**Specialties** (e.g. cleaning: standard, deep, move-out, post-construction, office, eco):
- are chosen on the application and in onboarding
- show in the Hub
- **change dispatch**: a pro whose specialty matches the booked service gets +8 dispatch score and is offered the job first

## 4. How we vet (live)

1. **Apply (5 min).** Trades, specialties, area, crew, equipment, coverages held, two references and links to their work.
2. **AI screen + call.** Our AI scores the application and flags gaps. A coordinator calls within 2 business days (IEBC recruiting agents Tyler Walsh / Marcus Hill).
3. **Skills check.** It is specific to each trade: photos of recent work, references and, for licensed trades, a lookup on the state license database (LARA).
4. **Documents.**
   - W-9 and contractor agreement
   - COI naming Handled as additional insured, verified with the carrier
   - license, if the trade needs one
   - any coverage the trade requires
5. **Background check.** Criminal and sex-offender search. Driving trades also get an MVR. It is re-run every year.
6. **Probation.** A pro's first **3 jobs** are capped at **$750** (dispatch enforces this). Each probation job also gets a human photo review and a customer follow-up call. The AI photo QA can't close a probation job: it raises a Hub alert, and staff approve it after the call.
7. **Activated.** Offers stop automatically when any required policy or license lapses.

**Keeping quality up:** ratings blend 60% customer and 40% Handled. AI photo QA runs on every job, and redos and refunds are tracked per pro on the Hub pro page.
- *Recommended rule:* coach a pro below 4.5★; pause them below 4.2★.

## 5. Insurance and licenses by trade (live; confirm with a broker and counsel)

**Everyone:** general liability **$1M per occurrence / $2M aggregate**, with Handled named as additional insured, plus a background check and a W-9.
**Workers' comp** is required if the pro has employees (Michigan law). Solo owners sign a no-employees statement in onboarding, which is stored and logged with their IP. **Remodelers always need a real workers' comp policy.**

| Trade | License | Extra coverage | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cleaning, organizing | — | **Fidelity bond** (unsupervised home access) | |
| Pet care | — | **Bond** | Liability must include care, custody & control of animals; pet first aid preferred |
| Tree service | — | **Commercial auto**, GL **$2M** | ISA Certified Arborist preferred; workers' comp nearly always |
| Transportation operators (black car, limo, party bus, coach, shuttle) | **MI passenger-carrier / limousine authority (MDOT); USDOT + FMCSA authority for interstate or 16+ seats.** Companies only | **Passenger-carrier auto: $1.5M (≤15 seats) / $5M (16+)**; workers' comp for employee drivers | CDL + passenger endorsement and DOT drug & alcohol testing for 16+ seat vehicles; driver MVRs |
| Junk hauling, roll-off containers, snow, rentals | — | **Commercial auto** ($1M CSL) | Personal auto policies usually exclude business use |
| Remodeling | **MI Residential Builder or Maintenance & Alteration license**, for work over $600 | **Workers' comp** | EPA RRP lead-safe certification for pre-1978 homes |
| Plumbing | **MI plumbing contractor license** | — | Master or journeyman on the job |
| Electrical / lighting | **MI electrical contractor license** | — | |
| HVAC | **MI mechanical contractor license + EPA 608** | — | NATE preferred |
| Painting (interior & exterior) | **MI Maintenance & Alteration (painting & decorating) or Residential Builder license**, for work over $600 | Workers' comp if employees | EPA RRP for pre-1978 homes; PDCA preferred |
| Cameras / low-voltage | — | — | Line-voltage work goes to a licensed electrician |
| Lawn | — | Auto if towing a trailer | MDARD applicator certification if they apply fertilizer or pesticide |
| Catering | **County food-service license + certified food manager** | Liquor liability if alcohol is served | |
| Food truck | **Mobile food license** | **Commercial auto**; liquor liability if alcohol is served | |
| Venue | — | GL **$2M**; liquor liability if alcohol is served | Certificate of occupancy showing capacity |

**How it's enforced:**
- Each required item is a step in the pro's onboarding checklist. Pros upload documents in `/pro/onboarding` and staff verify them in the Hub, which writes the expiry dates.
- Dispatch skips any pro whose required coverage is missing or expired.
- Remodel services are now marked "licensed", so they go only to pros with a license on file.

## 7. The six pay benefits (built 2026-10-01_2124 — you set who qualifies)

Open **Handled Hub → 🏅 Pro Program**. Each benefit has an on/off switch, a minimum tier, minimum jobs, minimum rating and the trades it covers, plus its own amounts. The page shows how many active pros qualify today. Only an admin can save changes, and they apply from the next job, cash-out or payout run. The defaults are below.

| # | Benefit | How it works | Default: who qualifies | Can it lose money? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Pay protection** | A refund that isn't the pro's fault comes out of our take first. Only what our take can't cover reduces the payout. | All active pros | No — capped at our take on that job |
| 2 | **Show-up pay** | On a late cancellation (inside 24h) or a confirmed lockout we keep the $49 fee, and the pro gets $35 of it automatically. | All active pros | No — the amount is cut if card costs would make the cancelled job negative |
| 3 | **Instant pay** | The pro connects Stripe once, then cashes out approved payouts any time for 1.5% (min $0.50). Otherwise the weekly payout run is free. | 5+ jobs | No — the fee covers Stripe's cost |
| 4 | **Insurance help** | Partner brokers are shown on /pros and in onboarding. The daily sweep automatically pays a one-time $150 stipend. | 10+ jobs and 4.6★ | Small fixed cost, only for proven pros |
| 5 | **Materials at cost** | The pro uploads a receipt. Up to $75 is approved automatically; above that staff approve. The customer is told and billed at cost, and **the pro is reimbursed after the customer pays**. Caps: 50% of the job price, or $300 for errand shopping. | Handyman, plumbing, electrical, HVAC, low-voltage, remodel, gutters, power washing, errands | No — pass-through |
| 6 | **Guaranteed weekly minimum** | Every Monday, qualifying pros who stayed available (5+ days, accepted 90%+ of offers) are topped up to $800 for the previous week. Each top-up waits for your approval in Finance and stops at a $2,000 weekly budget. | **Off.** When on: Elite pros in snow, lawn and cleaning, in Jan, Feb, May–Jul and Dec | Yes, by design — budget-capped, approval required |

**Also built:**
- **Cancellations:** customers cancel online (free 24h+ ahead; the fee applies inside 24h). Pros tap "Can't get in?", which alerts ops to call the customer. Staff cancel from the job page with a reason, and refunds (across the deposit and balance payments), the fee and show-up pay all happen in one step.
- **Agreements:** the contractor agreement is now v2, with a "Pro Program benefits" section, so pros re-sign in onboarding. The customer Service Agreement is v2: materials are billed at cost with a receipt, and the customer is told before the card is charged.
- **Stripe Connect:** turn on **Connect (Express)** in Stripe for instant pay. Pros onboard from **Earnings → Set up instant pay**.

## Pros vs employees

Pros don't pay us anything: no sign-up fee, no lead fees, no subscription. We keep a share (15–35%) of each prepaid job, and the pro gets the rest.

| | Handled pro (1099 independent business) | Regular worker (W-2 employee) |
|---|---|---|
| Who controls the work | The pro chooses which jobs, days, hours, area and daily limit, and can decline anything | The employer sets schedule and assignments |
| Pay | Per job, a fixed payout shown upfront; weekly, or instantly | Hourly or salary |
| Tools, vehicle, supplies | The pro's own (materials reimbursed at cost) | Usually provided |
| Insurance & license | The pro's own GL (plus trade coverage) and license | Employer's policies |
| Taxes | We issue a 1099; the pro pays their own self-employment tax | Employer withholds and pays payroll taxes |
| Benefits | Pro Program benefits (pay protection, show-up pay, stipend, tiers), no health/PTO | Employer benefits by law/policy |
| Other clients | Free to keep their own business and customers | Typically works only for the employer |

Keep it that way: never require pros to take a job, set their hours, or work only for us. That's why recurring visits and redos are *offered* first, not assigned.
