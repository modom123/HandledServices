Governed AI agent workforces for public agencies
Capability Statement
2026

IE Business Consultants (IEBC) builds and runs AI agent workforces that take on an agency's repetitive back-office and constituent-service work. Agency staff stay in control: each agent has a written role, scoped permissions and an autonomy level the agency sets. High-risk actions wait for a named official's approval, and every action is logged for audit.

Core competencies

  • Back-office automation: intake, forms and document extraction, case triage and routing, records clean-up.
  • Constituent service agents: 24/7 Tier-1/Tier-2 chat and email for 311 and service desks, multilingual, with handoff to staff.
  • Finance & audit automation: invoice audit, vendor reconciliation, grant reporting, improper-payment flags. A human certifies every payment.
  • Process mapping: turning manual SOPs into documented, measurable automated workflows (Lean Six Sigma method).
  • Data integration: read-only connectors first, then least-privilege write access to approved systems under the agency's ATO.
  • Capture & proposal automation: opportunity search, compliance matrices and bid workflows.

Differentiators

  • Proven in production: IEBC agents run dispatch, client care, compliance tracking and finance for a live multi-trade services company, Handled (affiliate).
  • Human oversight built in: three autonomy levels (suggest / act with approval / autonomous), an approval queue, and complete action logs. Designed around OMB AI use and acquisition guidance.
  • Fast, low-risk start: a fixed-price 30-day pilot with success measures agreed up front. A working proof of concept within 72 hours of data access.
  • No lock-in: agency data and workflows are exportable, and agency data is never used to train third-party models.
  • Local: Detroit-based small business.
