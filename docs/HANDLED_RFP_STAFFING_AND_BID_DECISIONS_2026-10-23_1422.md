## Rules that apply to every City job

- **Crews must be employees (W-2) or an insured subcontractor's employees.** Every City contract here requires Michigan workers' comp, and most ask for a named roster. Handled's usual 1099 "pro" model doesn't fit; a 1099 worker can't be on your workers' comp policy, and the City can't approve people it can't see on a roster.
- **Background checks for everyone.** DPD and DFD buildings require them; DDC requires CJIS clearance.
- **Hire Detroit residents.** The City gives more points for it, asks how many you'll use, and asks you to sign a Priority Hire Agreement with Detroit at Work (free recruiting help: detroitatwork.com). On construction-type work, Executive Order 2016-2 requires at least 50% of worker hours by Detroit residents.
- **Get CRIO certificates** (Detroit Headquartered, Detroit Resident Business, Detroit Micro Business). They add up to 10 points on RFPs and a 12% price allowance on low-bid RFQs.
- **Price the payroll right:** budget about **1.25–1.35×** the hourly wage for payroll taxes, workers' comp, and general liability insurance. Landscaping and demolition-type work has higher workers' comp rates.

**Where to find them:** your Indeed pipeline (landscapers, demolition and junk-removal workers translate directly), Detroit at Work, and the JV partner's existing crews. The partner's experienced supervisors and operators fill the senior slots; Handled hires and trains the laborers and crew leads.

## 3. DDC Facilities Maintenance (RFP 550148): why not, and what it would take

The work is licensed building-trades maintenance at the Detroit Detention Center: HVAC, plumbing, electrical, fire alarms and sprinklers, plus 24/7 emergency response (written acknowledgment within 2 hours, on site within 4 hours).

| Role it requires | License / requirement |
|---|---|
| Master or Journeyman Electrician (and the company's Michigan electrical contractor license) | State of Michigan license; 5 years of licensed experience |
| Master or Journeyman Plumber (and mechanical contractor license) | State of Michigan license; 5 years of licensed experience |
| HVAC lead technician | **1st Class Refrigeration Operator** license, **EPA Section 608 Universal** certification, 5 years of licensed experience |
| Fire alarm / sprinkler / kitchen-hood suppression technician | NFPA 72, 25, 17A and 80 work; typically NICET-certified and a state-licensed fire-protection contractor |
| 24/7 Project Manager | Available around the clock with full authority |
| 24-hour fire alarm monitoring | A monitoring center (usually a UL-listed central station) |
| Everyone on site | Must pass **CJIS** clearance and Michigan State Police background checks; no phones inside the facility |

The company itself also needs 5 years of experience in each trade and 5 references. This is a job for an established licensed mechanical/electrical contractor.

**What Handled can do instead:** contact licensed mechanical and electrical contractors that bid DDC and offer to be their subcontractor for unlicensed support work: general maintenance, cleaning, hauling, minor repairs. That builds a City track record without needing the trade licenses.

## 4. Bunker Gear Cleaning (RFP 551200): why not

This contract cleans, inspects and repairs firefighters' protective gear (about 2,400 coats and 2,400 pants a year) under NFPA 1850/1851 and 1855/1857. To bid, a company needs **all** of this already in place:

- A gear-care facility that is a **Verified Independent Service Provider** (UL or Intertek) with a certificate less than 2 years old, plus a **second verified backup facility**
- Industrial washer-extractors with **ozone infusion**, a **CO2 cleaning system** on site (supercritical CO2 within one year), and a hydrostatic tester for moisture barriers
- Third-party verification for Gore and Stedfast moisture-barrier materials, and **LION warranty repair authorization**
- The **PSTrax** PPE tracking module, a 24/7 hotline, an on-site quartermaster, loaner gear stock, and weekly pickup from every station
- 5 years of experience and 2 references; **no third parties allowed**

The staff would be trained ISP technicians, industrial sewing / repair technicians, a quartermaster, drivers and a 24/7 answering service. But the facility and verification come first, and they take a large investment and well over the 2 weeks left. Skip it.
