**Why this reads as a job:** a real title, specific duties, a schedule, pay per unit of work (per lead), and an
employer that provides the product, tools, training and support. The old post led with "be your own boss / commission
only / no quota", which is what Indeed flags as an opportunity.

**Talk to your attorney before posting.** Indeed's "Part-time/Full-time" job types and a set schedule can make the
role look like employment. If reps stay 1099, keep the hours their choice and show the pay as per lead + commission
(as below). Some states treat a per-lead minimum with set hours as wages. The surest way to pass Indeed's review is
an hourly base, W-2. If Indeed still flags the post, that's the next step.

**Field Sales Representative — Home & Business Services**
Handled · Detroit, MI · Part-time or full-time hours, your choice · $10 per lead + commission

**About the job**
Handled is a Detroit-area home and business services company: cleaning, lawn and snow, hauling, handyman, painting,
roofing, fences, remodels, websites and more, done by background-checked, vetted pros. We're hiring Field Sales
Representatives to find customers in their neighborhood, write up the job, and close the sale on the spot with our app.

**What you'll do**
- Meet homeowners, landlords and local businesses who need work done
- With their OK, take 3–4 photos and write a sales ticket in the Handled app (the service, sizes and timing)
- Show the customer their upfront price on the spot and help them book: they pay Handled on your phone or from our text
- Follow up on customers who need time to decide
- Share your referral link and code with realtors, property managers and neighbors

**Schedule**
Day, evening and weekend hours. Work part-time (10–20 hours a week) or full-time (30–40). You choose your hours.

**Pay**
- **$10 for every lead** (a complete sales ticket for a new customer), paid weekly.
- **Plus commission on every job you sell:** 10% of Handled's profit to start, rising one point at a time to 45%. Plus
  15% to 30% of the job price on customers you refer.
- Paid every Monday by direct deposit.
- How lead pay works: it's an advance on that lead's commission. When the lead's job pays, the $10 comes out of that
  commission; if the lead never books, you keep it. Lead pay starts after your first 10 leads and your first sale
  that's been paid out, and everything you built up before then is paid that week.
- Example: a $400 job where the pro is paid $300 earns you $10 at 10%, $45 at 45%. Roofs, remodels and commercial
  cleaning contracts pay much more.

*Earnings depend on the leads you write and the jobs you close; the figures above are examples, not a guarantee.*

**What we provide**
- The Handled app: instant pricing from photos, sales tickets, your pay and progress
- A short online orientation and sales talking points
- Background-checked, vetted pros who do the work, with photo proof and a free redo if anything's missed
- 100+ services, so almost everyone you talk to needs something

**Requirements**
- 18 or older
- Smartphone with a camera, and comfortable using apps
- Reliable transportation in the Detroit metro area
- Friendly, honest, and good at talking with people (sales or customer service experience a plus)
- Able to receive pay by direct deposit (bank account or debit card through Stripe)
- Pass a basic identity check

**How to apply**
Apply at the link (2 minutes). You'll get your app access, referral code and link right away.

`https://handledsvc.com/partners?kind=sales_rep&utm_source=indeed&utm_campaign=field_sales_job#join`

*Field Sales Representatives work as independent contractors (1099). We send a 1099-NEC if you earn $600+ in a year.*
