**Account Executive: Commercial & Home Services**
Handled · Detroit, MI · Independent contractor (1099) · Part-time or full-time · Commission + walkthrough pay + bonuses

**About Handled**
Handled is a commercial and home services company: office and facility cleaning, unit turnovers for property managers,
grounds, repairs, hauling and more, done by background-checked, vetted pros with photo proof on every visit.

**Two paths. Pick the one that fits.**
- **Path 1 · Refer:** send us customers and we sell and do the job. Earn 15% rising to 45% of our profit. Any amount of time.
- **Path 2 · Sell (this job):** close the work yourself as part of the Handled Sales Force. Earn 20% rising to 50% of our profit, plus walkthrough pay, bonuses and a career path from Associate AE to VP of Sales.

**Path 2 · Sell: the job (the Handled Sales Force)**
You find the work, close it, and get paid when it's done.
- Find offices, property managers, clinics, landlords and homeowners who need work done
- Book free walkthroughs (they pick a time online from your link) and send an exact price the same day
- Close the sale on the Handled app: the customer pays Handled directly, and you never handle money
- Keep your clients happy so they keep booking, and if you like, build a team of your own

**How you're paid** (every Monday, by direct deposit)
- **20% of Handled's profit on every sale to start, rising to 50%** as the profit you make us grows
- **$25 for every walkthrough held** from your link, and **$100 for your first sale**
- **Your book:** keep earning on your clients after their first year
- **A cash bonus with every promotion**, and team pay when you lead

**Your title and your rate on a sale**
| Title | Your rate on a sale |
|---|---|
| Associate Account Executive (where everyone starts) | 20% |
| Account Executive | 22% |
| Senior Account Executive | 25% |
| Principal Account Executive | 35% |
| Team Lead → Sales Manager → Director → VP of Sales | 30% → 45%, plus a share of your team's sales |

Promotions come from the total profit your clients have made Handled since your first day, plus a minimum number of
deals and clients won. Totals never reset, so you never lose a title. Your rate keeps rising with that profit, up to 50%.

**Path 1 · Refer: if you'd rather not sell**
Know people who need work done but don't want to sell? Join free as a referral partner instead: share your link, we
call, quote and do the job, and you earn **15% rising to 45% of our profit** on every job from your customers for
12 months. Sign up at handledsvc.com/partners and choose "Path 1 · Refer".

**How you work**
You're an independent contractor: you choose your hours, where you sell and how you sell. No quotas, no required
schedule. Free, optional training and a mentor if you want them. We provide the pricing app, landing pages, walkthrough
booking and warm leads from our AI outreach.

**Requirements**
18+, a smartphone, reliable transportation, a bank account for direct deposit, and a basic identity check.

**To apply:** go to handledsvc.com/careers/sales and choose "Path 2 · Sell" when you sign up. It takes two minutes.

*Independent contractor (1099). We send a 1099-NEC if you earn $600+ in a year. Earnings depend on what you sell.*
