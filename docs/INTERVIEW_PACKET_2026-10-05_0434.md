# Pro interview packet

Screening interview for independent pros (1099 businesses). The same questions and scoring are used by the AI
interviewer, the Hub scorecard and the printable page (Hub → Recruiting → Interview packet). **A person makes every
decision.** Say "approved to receive job offers", not "hired".

## 1. How a pro gets approved
1. Applies at /pros. An AI screens their business qualifications.
2. **Screening interview:** either the AI interviewer (a private link by email, about 15 minutes, English or Spanish) or a person, in person or by phone.
3. **A person decides:** invite, follow up, or decline.
4. **Setup in the pro portal:**
   - W-9
   - independent contractor agreement
   - specialties
   - work area and days
   - insurance certificate (general liability, plus trade coverages)
   - workers' comp or a no-employees statement
   - trade license where required
   - photo ID check
   - background check
   - payout method
5. We verify the documents and the background check clears. They're then **approved to receive job offers**.
6. **First jobs are probation:** smaller jobs, a human review of each one, and a call to the customer.

## 2. Never ask about
- Age or date of birth / _Edad o fecha de nacimiento_
- Race, color, ethnicity, national origin, birthplace or accent / _Raza, color, etnia, origen nacional, lugar de nacimiento o acento_
- Citizenship or immigration status / _Ciudadanía o estatus migratorio_
- Religion or religious holidays / _Religión o días religiosos_
- Sex, gender identity, sexual orientation / _Sexo, identidad de género, orientación sexual_
- Marriage, pregnancy, children or childcare / _Matrimonio, embarazo, hijos o cuidado de niños_
- Disability, health, medical history, medications, workers' comp claims / _Discapacidad, salud, historial médico, medicamentos, reclamos de compensación laboral_
- Height, weight or genetic information / _Estatura, peso o información genética_
- Arrests or criminal history (the background check handles this, with notice and a chance to respond) / _Arrestos o antecedentes penales (la verificación de antecedentes se encarga de esto, con aviso y oportunidad de responder)_
- Military discharge type / _Tipo de baja militar_
- Credit, debts, bankruptcy, or past pay / _Crédito, deudas, bancarrota o pagos anteriores_
- Union membership or political views / _Afiliación sindical u opiniones políticas_
- Language ability beyond what the job needs (we work in English and Spanish) / _Habilidad con idiomas más allá de lo que el trabajo requiere (trabajamos en inglés y español)_

If a candidate brings one of these up, don't follow up and don't let it affect the decision. Score what they said,
not their accent, grammar or which language they use. Say yes to accommodation requests, such as more time, a phone
interview or an interpreter.

## 3. Questions for everyone
1. **Tell me about your business and the work you do most.**  
   _ES: Cuénteme sobre su negocio y el trabajo que hace con más frecuencia._  
   ✔ Look for: Specific services, who their customers are, how long they've done it.  
   Scores: Skill & experience
2. **How long have you done this work, and how did you learn it?**  
   _ES: ¿Cuánto tiempo lleva haciendo este trabajo y cómo lo aprendió?_  
   ✔ Look for: Years and real examples; apprenticeships, past companies or family business all count.  
   Scores: Skill & experience
3. **Describe a recent job you're proud of. What made it good?**  
   _ES: Describa un trabajo reciente del que esté orgulloso. ¿Qué lo hizo bueno?_  
   ✔ Look for: Concrete details; talks about the result for the customer, not just speed.  
   Scores: Quality standards
4. **Before you call a job done, how do you check your own work?**  
   _ES: Antes de dar un trabajo por terminado, ¿cómo revisa su propio trabajo?_  
   ✔ Look for: A walkthrough, photos, a list — some habit of checking.  
   ✖ Red flag: "I just know" / no habit of checking.  
   Scores: Quality standards
5. **Tell me about a time a customer wasn't happy with the work. What did you do?**  
   _ES: Cuénteme de una vez que un cliente no quedó contento con el trabajo. ¿Qué hizo?_  
   ✔ Look for: Listened, fixed it, kept calm; owns mistakes.  
   ✖ Red flag: Blames the customer, argues, or walks away.  
   Scores: Customer communication
6. **You're going to be 30 minutes late to a job. What do you do?**  
   _ES: Va a llegar 30 minutos tarde a un trabajo. ¿Qué hace?_  
   ✔ Look for: Tells the customer before the window starts, gives a real ETA.  
   ✖ Red flag: Doesn't tell anyone, or thinks lateness is normal.  
   Scores: Reliability
7. **Which areas and days do you want jobs in, and how many jobs a day can you do well?**  
   _ES: ¿En qué zonas y días quiere trabajos, y cuántos trabajos al día puede hacer bien?_  
   ✔ Look for: Realistic capacity; knows their own limits. (They set this themselves.)  
   Scores: Reliability
8. **What would make you cancel a job you'd already accepted, and how would you handle it?**  
   _ES: ¿Qué haría que cancelara un trabajo que ya aceptó, y cómo lo manejaría?_  
   ✔ Look for: Only real emergencies; gives as much notice as possible.  
   ✖ Red flag: Would drop a job for a better-paying one.  
   Scores: Reliability
9. **At a job, the customer asks you to "just do one more thing" that wasn't booked. What do you do?**  
   _ES: En un trabajo, el cliente le pide que "haga una cosa más" que no estaba reservada. ¿Qué hace?_  
   ✔ Look for: Explains it's extra and sends a change order through the app before doing it.  
   ✖ Red flag: Does it off the books for cash.  
   Scores: Customer communication
10. **A customer offers you cash to come back next month without Handled. What do you say?**  
   _ES: Un cliente le ofrece efectivo para regresar el próximo mes sin Handled. ¿Qué le dice?_  
   ✔ Look for: Declines politely and keeps it on the app (their agreement has a 12-month non-solicit).  
   ✖ Red flag: Would take the cash job.  
   Scores: Business readiness
11. **How do you protect a customer's home and belongings while you work?**  
   _ES: ¿Cómo protege la casa y las pertenencias del cliente mientras trabaja?_  
   ✔ Look for: Covers floors, moves things carefully, asks before touching valuables.  
   Scores: Safety & property care
12. **You find something unsafe at a job — exposed wires, mold, a ladder or roof you don't trust. What do you do?**  
   _ES: Encuentra algo inseguro en un trabajo: cables expuestos, moho, una escalera o techo que no le da confianza. ¿Qué hace?_  
   ✔ Look for: Stops that part, tells the customer and us, doesn't work beyond their license.  
   ✖ Red flag: Pushes through anyway.  
   Scores: Safety & property care
13. **Our jobs use a phone app: you accept offers, follow a checklist and upload before-and-after photos. How comfortable are you with that?**  
   _ES: Nuestros trabajos usan una app: acepta ofertas, sigue una lista y sube fotos de antes y después. ¿Qué tan cómodo se siente con eso?_  
   ✔ Look for: Uses a smartphone daily, or has someone on their team who does.  
   Scores: Business readiness
14. **Do you carry general liability insurance today? If not, are you willing to get it before your first job?**  
   _ES: ¿Tiene hoy un seguro de responsabilidad civil general? Si no, ¿está dispuesto a obtenerlo antes de su primer trabajo?_  
   ✔ Look for: Yes, or a clear yes to getting it (we help with quotes).  
   ✖ Red flag: Won't carry insurance.  
   Scores: Business readiness
15. **What tools, equipment and transportation do you bring to a job?**  
   _ES: ¿Qué herramientas, equipo y transporte lleva a un trabajo?_  
   ✔ Look for: Own tools and a way to get to jobs (and haul, if their trade needs it).  
   Scores: Business readiness
16. **Do you work alone or with a crew? If you have helpers, who pays them and covers their insurance?**  
   _ES: ¿Trabaja solo o con un equipo? Si tiene ayudantes, ¿quién les paga y cubre su seguro?_  
   ✔ Look for: Clear: their business pays and covers its own crew (workers' comp when they have employees).  
   Scores: Business readiness
17. **What questions do you have for us?**  
   _ES: ¿Qué preguntas tiene para nosotros?_  
   ✔ Look for: Not scored. Good questions about how offers, pay and support work are a plus.  
   Scores: not scored

## 4. Questions by trade
### cleaning
1. **Walk me through how a deep clean of a bathroom differs from a standard clean.**  
   _ES: Explíqueme en qué se diferencia una limpieza profunda de un baño de una limpieza estándar._  
   ✔ Look for: Grout, glass and tracks, fixtures, baseboards, vents — and the time it takes.  
   Scores: Skill & experience
2. **Which products or methods do you avoid on natural stone, hardwood or stainless steel?**  
   _ES: ¿Qué productos o métodos evita en piedra natural, madera o acero inoxidable?_  
   ✔ Look for: Knows acids/abrasives damage stone and finishes; tests in a hidden spot.  
   Scores: Safety & property care

### repair
1. **A door sticks and won't latch. How do you figure out what's wrong?**  
   _ES: Una puerta se atora y no cierra. ¿Cómo averigua qué está mal?_  
   ✔ Look for: Checks hinges and screws, strike plate, frame / settling — before planing.  
   Scores: Skill & experience
2. **When do you tell a customer a job needs a licensed electrician or plumber instead of you?**  
   _ES: ¿Cuándo le dice a un cliente que un trabajo necesita un electricista o plomero con licencia en lugar de usted?_  
   ✔ Look for: Knows the line: new circuits, gas, main lines, permits.  
   ✖ Red flag: Would do licensed work without a license.  
   Scores: Safety & property care

### outdoor
1. **How do you make a yard look finished when you leave?**  
   _ES: ¿Cómo deja un jardín con aspecto de terminado al irse?_  
   ✔ Look for: Edging, trimming around obstacles, blowing walks and drive, clippings off the street.  
   Scores: Quality standards
2. **How do you keep rocks and debris from hitting cars, windows and people?**  
   _ES: ¿Cómo evita que piedras y desechos golpeen autos, ventanas y personas?_  
   ✔ Look for: Walks the yard first, discharge direction, stops when people pass.  
   Scores: Safety & property care

### moving
1. **How do you move a heavy dresser down a flight of stairs safely?**  
   _ES: ¿Cómo baja un tocador pesado por una escalera de forma segura?_  
   ✔ Look for: Empty it, two people, straps/dolly, the strong person below, protect walls and floors.  
   Scores: Safety & property care
2. **You scratch a customer's floor while moving furniture. What do you do?**  
   _ES: Raya el piso de un cliente al mover un mueble. ¿Qué hace?_  
   ✔ Look for: Tells the customer right away, photos, reports it — never hides it.  
   ✖ Red flag: Hides it.  
   Scores: Customer communication

### painting
1. **What prep do you do before painting a room?**  
   _ES: ¿Qué preparación hace antes de pintar un cuarto?_  
   ✔ Look for: Move/cover furniture and floors, patch, sand, clean, tape or cut in, prime where needed.  
   Scores: Skill & experience
2. **What changes when the home was built before 1978?**  
   _ES: ¿Qué cambia cuando la casa fue construida antes de 1978?_  
   ✔ Look for: Lead paint: EPA RRP certified firm, containment, no dry sanding.  
   Scores: Safety & property care

### licensed
1. **Which licenses do you hold, in which city or state, and when do they expire?**  
   _ES: ¿Qué licencias tiene, en qué ciudad o estado, y cuándo vencen?_  
   ✔ Look for: Specific license types and numbers they can provide for verification.  
   Scores: Business readiness
2. **When do you pull a permit, and how do you handle inspections?**  
   _ES: ¿Cuándo saca un permiso y cómo maneja las inspecciones?_  
   ✔ Look for: Knows local rules; schedules inspections; doesn't skip them.  
   ✖ Red flag: Avoids permits.  
   Scores: Safety & property care

### pets
1. **A dog slips out of its harness on a walk. What do you do?**  
   _ES: Un perro se zafa del arnés en un paseo. ¿Qué hace?_  
   ✔ Look for: Stays calm, doesn't chase into traffic, calls the owner and us right away.  
   Scores: Safety & property care
2. **During a visit, a pet seems sick. What do you do?**  
   _ES: Durante una visita, una mascota parece enferma. ¿Qué hace?_  
   ✔ Look for: Calls the owner, follows their vet instructions, documents it.  
   Scores: Customer communication

### transport
1. **Who is the licensed operator for your vehicles, and what licenses and endorsements do your drivers hold?**  
   _ES: ¿Quién es el operador con licencia de sus vehículos, y qué licencias y endosos tienen sus conductores?_  
   ✔ Look for: Named licensed operator; CDL / chauffeur license as required; commercial auto insurance.  
   Scores: Business readiness
2. **A passenger is intoxicated and getting aggressive. What do you do?**  
   _ES: Un pasajero está ebrio y se pone agresivo. ¿Qué hace?_  
   ✔ Look for: Stays calm, stops safely, doesn't escalate, calls for help when needed and reports it.  
   Scores: Safety & property care

### errands
1. **How do you prove a delivery was made, and what do you do when nobody's there to receive it?**  
   _ES: ¿Cómo comprueba que se hizo una entrega y qué hace cuando no hay nadie para recibirla?_  
   ✔ Look for: Photo, signature when required, follows instructions, never leaves sensitive items unattended.  
   Scores: Quality standards

### events
1. **On event day you're running late or a piece of equipment fails. What do you do?**  
   _ES: El día del evento va tarde o falla un equipo. ¿Qué hace?_  
   ✔ Look for: Early arrival buffer, backup equipment, tells the planner right away.  
   Scores: Reliability

## 5. Scoring guide (1–5)
| Competency | 1 — concerning | 3 — meets | 5 — excellent |
|---|---|---|---|
| Skill & experience | Vague or no real experience in the trades they chose | Clear, relevant experience; explains how they do common jobs | Deep experience; explains trade-offs and edge cases like a pro |
| Quality standards | "Good enough"; no way of checking their work | Checks their own work; takes photos; fixes misses | Clear personal standard, walkthroughs, owns and fixes issues without being asked |
| Reliability | Casual about lateness or cancelling; no plan when things change | Tells the customer early when running late; realistic about capacity | Plans ahead, rarely cancels, has a backup when something goes wrong |
| Customer communication | Argues, blames, or would do unpaid / off-app work | Polite, explains clearly, uses change orders for extra work | Calm under pressure, sets expectations, turns complaints into return customers |
| Safety & property care | Ignores hazards or would push through unsafe work | Protects floors and belongings; stops when something is unsafe | Proactive about hazards, knows the code / limits of their trade, protects people and property |
| Business readiness | No insurance and unwilling to get it; no tools or transportation | Has or will get insurance; own tools and transportation; can use a phone app | Insured and licensed where needed, set up as a business, comfortable with app, photos and receipts |

**How the result is worked out:**
- **Advance:** the average of the six scores is 3.2 or more, and no score is below 2.
- **Follow up:** any score is very low, or a competency wasn't covered.
- **Not now:** the average is under 2.6, or there's a knockout.

**Knockouts:**
- won't carry required insurance
- would do licensed work without the license
- would knowingly continue unsafe work
- abusive or threatening in the interview

## 6. What the AI interviewer may tell candidates
- Handled sends paid, upfront-priced job offers to independent pros through an app. You choose which jobs to take; passing never counts against you.
- Pros are independent businesses (1099), not employees. You set your own days, area and daily job limit, and use your own methods and tools.
- The pay for each job is shown before you accept. Pay is sent weekly; instant cash-out is optional for a small fee. Any pay figures before you're approved are estimates.
- To be approved: this interview, a W-9, the independent contractor agreement, general liability insurance (and trade licenses where required), a photo ID check and a background check.
- Jobs come with a checklist and need before-and-after photos in the app.
- If you have a crew, your business pays and directs them and handles their insurance; each person who goes into a home passes our background check.
- We work in English and Spanish.

**How the AI interviewer is set up:**
- It says it's an AI before starting, and the candidate agrees before it begins.
- The candidate can ask for a person instead at any point.
- A person reviews every interview and makes the decision.
- Scores come with quoted evidence, and the result is computed from the rubric above.
