# Factoring outreach emails

**Who gets it:** the five companies on the **Comparison** tab of `FACTORING_COMPARISON_2026-10-06_0320.xlsx`
(Advance Partners, 1st Commercial Credit, Porter Capital, eCapital, 8A Factoring). Send the first email
through each company's contact or "get a quote" form, or to their sales email.

**Before sending:** replace every `[bracket]`. Use the same sample invoice figures for all five so the quotes
compare cleanly in the **Cost Calculator** tab.

---

## Email 1: Quote request

**Subject:** Factoring quote request: Handled Services LLC (net-30 business and government invoices)

Hello [Name / team],

My name is [Your name], and I am the [title] of **Handled Services LLC**, a Detroit-based company that provides
cleaning, landscaping, maintenance and related services to homes, businesses and public agencies.

We are expanding into larger commercial accounts and city and government contracts, which typically pay on
**net 30 to net 60** terms. Our service pros are paid **weekly**, so we are looking for a factoring partner
to cover that gap. I would like a quote and your terms for the following:

- **Clients:** commercial businesses, the City of Detroit and other municipalities, the State of Michigan, and
  federal agencies (SAM.gov), as prime contractor or subcontractor
- **Expected monthly invoice volume:** [$ amount] to start, growing to [$ amount] within [months]
- **Typical invoice size:** [$ amount]; typical payment terms: [net 30 / 45 / 60]
- **Time in business / annual revenue:** [years] / [$ amount]

Could you please tell us:

1. Your advance rate and fee per 30 days (or per 10 or 15 days), and how the fee changes with volume
2. Whether the agreement is recourse or non-recourse
3. Any monthly minimums, the contract term, and the early-termination fee
4. Whether we can choose which invoices or clients to factor (spot factoring)
5. All other fees (setup, due diligence, ACH/wire, credit checks, lockbox)
6. Whether you fund federal, state and **municipal** receivables, and how you handle the Assignment of Claims
   on federal contracts and consent requirements on city contracts
7. How quickly you fund after an invoice is verified, and the documents you need to open an account

To compare like for like, could you also show the total cost on a sample **$[50,000] invoice paid in [30] days**?

I am available for a call at [phone] or at [email]. Thank you, and I look forward to hearing from you.

Best regards,
[Your name]
[Title], Handled Services LLC
[Phone] · [Email] · [Website]

## After each reply

1. Enter the advance rate, fee and terms in that company's row on the **Comparison** tab and set the status.
2. Check the **Cost Calculator** tab. Compare *total cost* and *cash up front*, not the headline rate alone.
3. Go through the **Questions & Gov Rules** tab on the call and note anything they did not answer.
4. Before signing, have the agreement reviewed, especially the UCC lien, recourse, minimums and exit fee.
